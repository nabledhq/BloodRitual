# 0012. Weapons, health and a first infected

Status: Accepted

## Context

Part 2 of the vertical slice (ADR 0009) covers the canoe, weapons and the
infected. The owner asked for weapons first: a rifle, a bow, a melee weapon
and a rock sledgehammer, hurting something real (a first infected enemy), with
rifle rounds and arrows coming out of the Ammo the player loots. The settings
already held the bow and rifle noise radii for this.

The constraints carried over from ADR 0009 and ADR 0011:
* Art has to be CC0 or the project's own.
* Binary content has to stay under 50 MB (45.6 MB was used).
* There is no Animation Blueprint yet.
* The map is spawned at runtime, so it has no NavMesh.
* StateTree is the planned AI framework (ADR 0003).

## Decision

* **Health.** `UBloodRitualHealthComponent` (`Survival/`) on anything that can
  be hurt. It receives damage through the engine's damage pipeline
  (`UGameplayStatics::ApplyDamage`, then the owner's `OnTakeAnyDamage`) and
  broadcasts `OnDamaged` and `OnDied`. This is plain C++, as ADR 0007 decided.
* **Weapons.** `UBloodRitualWeaponComponent` (`Combat/`) on the player holds
  the weapons, the rifle's loaded rounds and every attack. Its numbers are in
  `UBloodRitualSettings`.
  * **Tomahawk and rock sledgehammer.** A swing lands at the swing clip's
    contact time and hurts every living pawn in an arc in front of him; the
    sledgehammer is slower, stronger and knocks back.
  * **Rifle.** Hitscan along the camera's view, with an eight-round magazine
    that R refills from carried Ammo.
  * **Bow.** Each arrow costs one carried Ammo and flies as an
    `ABloodRitualArrow`, which damages what it hits and sticks in it.
  * **Headshots.** A hit in the top 28 cm of a target's capsule does double
    damage.
  * **Noise.** Shots and landed blows report noise (rifle 3000 uu, bow 500 uu,
    melee 600 uu), so the noise system now matters.
* **Held weapons** are static meshes modelled in the Protagonist's mesh space
  at his rest pose, in a grip frame taken from his hand bones. They are
  attached to `hand_r`, offset by the inverse of that bone's reference
  transform.
* **The infected.** `ABloodRitualInfected` (the body: health, hearing as an
  `IBloodRitualNoiseListener`, the blow, the flinch and death) is driven by
  `ABloodRitualInfectedController` (`AI/`). The controller runs the documented
  loop (Wander, Investigate, Chase with Attack, Search) as a small C++ state
  machine, not StateTree yet, keeping the same states so it can move there.
  Its senses:
  * sight: a 120° cone to 16 m with a line-of-sight trace;
  * proximity: 3 m in any direction;
  * noise;
  * being hurt.

  It steers straight at its goal, sidesteps when blocked and uses RVO
  avoidance against other infected, because the map has no NavMesh. The test
  environment spawns `InfectedCount` infected around the scavenging area.
* **Art within the budget.**
  * **Weapons** are generated by `scripts/blender/build_weapons.py` (lofted
    shapes and one 256 px atlas), 0.17 MB in all.
  * **The infected** (`scripts/blender/build_infected.py`) use MakeHuman's
    CC0 1591-vertex male proxy body with the Protagonist's phenotype and rig.
    They are imported onto his skeleton, play all his clips, and reuse his
    skin and eye textures (desaturated and tinted through a new
    `Desaturation` parameter on the character master materials), plus
    generated rags; 0.69 MB in all.
  * **Animations:** nine more Quaternius clips (sword idle and swing, pistol
    idle, aim, shoot and reload, punch, hit, death) and two blend spaces with
    armed idles.

  Content totals 48.2 MB.
* **Animation stays single-node.** A swing, shot, reload or flinch is a
  full-body one-shot that holds him in place briefly. Aiming plays the aim
  pose when he stands still. The rifle and bow borrow the pistol clips and
  the melee weapons the sword clips; no CC0 rifle or bow animations exist.
* **Death and respawn.** At zero health the player plays the death clip and,
  after `PlayerRespawnDelaySeconds`, the game mode restarts him at the
  PlayerStart. His carried supplies are lost and he respawns with
  `StartingAmmo`. Dead infected stay without collision for
  `InfectedCorpseSeconds`.

## Consequences

* The slice has a playable combat loop:
  * loot Ammo, then shoot or fight;
  * the noise draws the infected, who chase him and fight back;
  * he dies and respawns at camp.

  Automation tests cover health, weapon ammunition and the infected's senses
  and states.
* Known limitations until the Animation Blueprint and AI tickets:
  * Without blending or upper-body layering he cannot attack while moving,
    and switching animations cuts abruptly.
  * The rifle is held one-handed like a pistol, and the bow is raised without
    a draw.
  * The infected share the Protagonist's proportions (on a slimmer proxy
    body).
  * Without a NavMesh they can get stuck behind large obstacles.
  * There is no sound and no ragdoll.
* With 48.2 MB of 50 MB used, the next art (the canoe, more enemy types)
  needs budget: trimmed assets, a raised limit, or content kept out of Git.
* ADR 0003 still stands: the infected's brain is meant to become a
  StateTree, with AI Perception and NavMesh movement, when the AI ticket is
  funded.
