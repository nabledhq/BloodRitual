# Community

The player camp as a community: villagers, their roles and needs, and camp upgrades between day and night phases.

Implemented (vertical slice part 1): `ABloodRitualStockpile`, the hub supply store. `DepositAll` takes a whole inventory, `TrySpend(Type, Amount)` is what camp upgrades and barricades draw from. Villagers and roles are later.
See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
