# Asset licences

Every texture, environment map and model in Seminole is listed here with its
source and licence. Third-party assets must be CC0 (or similarly
unrestricted), no larger than 2048×2048 per texture, and the total size of
added binary assets must stay under 50 MB.

## Third-party assets

The villagers (NPCs) are premade, rigged human models with professional
animation clips by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain,
no attribution required; credited here anyway). The free "Standard" versions
were used, fetched from a public GitHub mirror of the unchanged glTF files
(`shellybotmoyer/stargate-universe`, commit `84e7b1a`, folder
`models/quaternius/`) because the original download pages need a browser.
`tools/npc-assets/fetch-sources.sh` downloads exactly those files and
`tools/npc-assets/build.mjs` turns them into the two files below
(the head of a base character joined to the parts of an outfit on one
skeleton, unused vertex attributes removed, textures downscaled to at most
1024×1024 JPEG, Ranger green recoloured to buckskin brown, animation
scale tracks and non-hip translation tracks removed).

| `npc-characters.glb` (5.3 MB) | Models `man`, `woman`, `hunter`: heads, eyes and eyebrows from the base characters; hair styles and beard | Universal Base Characters (Quaternius) | https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html · https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters | CC0 1.0 |
| `npc-characters.glb` | Peasant outfit (shirt, arms, trousers, shoes; male and female) and Ranger outfit (tunic, arms, legs, boots; recoloured) with their textures | Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (Quaternius) | https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html · https://quaternius.itch.io/modular-character-outfits-fantasy | CC0 1.0 |
| `npc-animations.glb` (1.3 MB) | 40 clips (locomotion, sitting, crouching, carrying, talking, farming, chopping, fixing, eating, combat, death, idle variants) on the universal humanoid rig | Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2, Standard (Quaternius) | https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html · https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library · https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library-2 | CC0 1.0 |

Total third-party binary size: 6.5 MB (largest texture 1024×1024).

project's [MIT licence](LICENSE).
