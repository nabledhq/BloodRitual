# Asset licences

Every texture, environment map and model in Seminole is listed here with its
source and licence. Third-party assets must be CC0 (or similarly
unrestricted), no larger than 2048×2048 per texture, and the total size of
added binary assets must stay under 50 MB.

## Third-party assets

_None._ The game does not ship or download any third-party textures, HDRIs or
models.

## Project assets (this repository, MIT)

_None yet._ `Content/` is empty: the Unreal project spawns engine primitives
(cubes, cylinders, spheres) with engine materials at runtime and stores no
binary assets of its own (0 MB added).
